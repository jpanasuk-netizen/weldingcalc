How a row was chosen

A row is one published process at one published input voltage. The Power MIG 210 MP brochure also names flux-cored, DC stick, and DC TIG. Its product-specifications table rates MIG only, so those other processes are not rows. The Square Wave TIG 200 sheet’s 120 V TIG line is marked with a note that 120 V AC TIG is 110A/25%. That note is in the cell.

Frequently asked questions

What does the duty tab print for the Handler 140 nameplate at 100 amps?

Enter 125 mils on the MIG tab. The printed window starts at 100 A. On the duty tab, enter rated amps 90, rated duty 20, and actual amps 100. The duty tab prints 16 percent: 1.6 minutes on and 8.4 minutes off. That is 20 × (90 ÷ 100) squared, which is 16.2, rounded to 16. The Handler 140 sheet rates 90 A at 18.5 VDC, 20% duty cycle. The duty tab does not take the 18.5 V figure.

What if a sheet names a process and does not rate it?

That process is not a row. This table does not estimate a duty cycle, and it does not fill a blank with a guess.
