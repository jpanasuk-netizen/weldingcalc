Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

WeldingCalc (welding-calculator.com) is a free welding calculator site owned by Jeremy Panasuk, New Auburn, Wisconsin. Contact: jpanasuk@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The values you enter (thickness, amperage, wire diameter, and similar inputs) are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. They exist only in your browser session. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails, or personal profiles through the calculators.

Scripts this site loads

Pages load first-party files from this domain: assets/styles.css, and on some pages assets/app.js and assets/monetization.js. Those scripts do not send calculator inputs to a server. On the homepage, assets/monetization.js stores one value in your browser’s session storage so it can remember whether you dismissed the Amazon link bar. That value stays on your device.

Advertising

This site does not load Google AdSense or any other advertising script.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to Amazon.com (Amazon Associates), using the tag generatorsi0d-20. If you follow an affiliate link and buy something, the site may earn a commission. Amazon can set its own cookies after you leave this site. Affiliate links do not change the calculator math.

Other outbound links

The homepage and topic map include a plain link to tiktok.com/@3jsa7. That link is in the page HTML. This site does not embed TikTok or load a TikTok script.

Analytics and cookies

We do not run our own analytics and we do not set our own tracking cookies.
