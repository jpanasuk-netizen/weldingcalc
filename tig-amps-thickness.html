Where the 1 A / 0.001" rule breaks

Aluminum: oxide layer eats heat on AC — run ~1.5× and use a #7 or larger cup with argon. Thin stainless: heat discolors and warps fast; stay at the bottom of the range and back the pedal off mid-bead. Over 1/4": preheat to 200–300°F or no rule survives the puddle.

AC balance for aluminum

Modern inverters: start at 65–70% electrode negative, 100–120 Hz frequency for a tight arc on 0.062–0.125". Cleaning action too wide means too much electrode positive and a balling tungsten.
